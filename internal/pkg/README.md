# Panix internal/pkg

Internal packages in this (`/internal/pkg`) directory are licensed under [MIT](LICENSE).

They are intentionally not licensed the same as the rest of the repository (which is [AGPL-3.0](../../LICENSE)), due to them having more generic properties and potentially being helpful to others if they are published under a more permissive license.

They are not published as libraries under `/pkg` as they may change or be removed without notice.

While most are implemented to be generic, some are tied/hardcoded for the specific implementations of Panix.
